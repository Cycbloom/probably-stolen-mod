[h1]垃圾场拓展：分区拾荒与声望成长[/h1]

把垃圾场变成可以按需求探索的地方。在八个特色区域中选择目的地，寻找材料、工具、食品、药品、电子零件或水处理用品，并通过拾荒积累声望。

[h2]主要内容[/h2]
[list]
[*]地图点击选区，地标支持悬停反馈，各区域拥有对应场景与物资倾向。
[*]每周区域事件带来不同增益，地图和告示牌显示当天活动。
[*]大奖与小奖使用独立的动态概率：大奖按天增长，小奖按搜索增长，中奖后降低。
[*]告示牌可查看物品概率，以及基础、贵重品、箱卡、事件和大小奖的期望收益拆分。
[*]废泵站可找到带水的储水容器，水量、水质和设备剩余使用量各有差异。
[*]可选「拾荒客」开局，附标准储藏区与工具，每趟增加2次拾荒。
[*]提供默认/加强预设，支持调整掉落、奖励与区域专精。
[/list]

[h2]声望与解锁[/h2]
[h3]区域开放[/h3]
0：入口空地、生活废料带。
20：拆解堆、废泵站。
40：报废车场、医疗废弃点。
60：电子垃圾坡、废武器堆。
80：开放贵重品掉落。
100：开放补给箱、对应钥匙卡及次日同行服务。

[h3]基础收益与同行服务[/h3]
20/40/60声望时，所有已开放区域的基础收益目标分别累计提高10%/20%/30%，大小奖另算。旧地点会同步成长，可以继续按物资需求选择区域。
100声望的同行服务每次花费20信用点：次日增加2次拾荒，并免受拾荒轻伤和重伤。

[h2]使用方法[/h2]
订阅下载完成后，在游戏模组菜单启用并重启。夜间前往垃圾场，选择本次作业区域即可。新开局可选「拾荒客」；已有存档也可使用选区和声望系统。
使用原生模组加载器，无强制前置。曾手动安装的玩家请移开本地同ID副本，避免覆盖工坊版。

[h1]Scrapyard Expansion: Regional Scavenging & Reputation[/h1]

Choose where to scavenge in an expanded scrapyard. Explore eight distinct areas for materials, tools, food, medicine, electronics or water-treatment supplies, and build reputation through scavenging.

[h2]Main Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Click the overview map to select an area, with responsive landmarks, regional artwork and distinct loot preferences.
[*]Weekly regional events provide different bonuses. Active events appear on the map and notice board.
[*]Jackpots and minor rewards have separate adaptive odds: jackpots grow by day, minor rewards by search, and wins reduce their odds.
[*]The notice board shows item probabilities and separate expected-value contributions from basic loot, valuables, crates/keycards, events and rewards.
[*]Find water-filled containers at the Abandoned Pump Station, with varied fill levels, water quality and remaining equipment uses.
[*]Optional Scavenger starting scenario with a standard Storage Bay, tools and two extra searches per visit.
[*]Default/Enhanced presets and configurable loot, rewards and regional specialization.
[/list]

[h2]Reputation & Unlocks[/h2]
[h3]Area Unlocks[/h3]
0: Main Gate and Refuse Lane.
20: Scrap Pile and Abandoned Pump Station.
40: Vehicle Graveyard and Medical Waste.
60: E-Waste Slope and Weapon Dump.
80: Valuable drops unlock.
100: Supply crates, matching keycards and the next-day outing service unlock.

[h3]Base Rewards & Outing Service[/h3]
At 20/40/60 reputation, all open areas gain cumulative base-reward targets of +10%/+20%/+30%. Jackpots and minor rewards are separate. Earlier areas improve alongside progression, so you can keep choosing destinations for the supplies you need.
At 100 reputation, the outing service costs 20 credits for two extra searches and protection from minor and major scavenging wounds on the following day.

[h2]How to Play[/h2]
After subscribing and downloading, enable the mod in the game's Mods menu and restart. Visit the dump at night and select your area. Scavenger is an optional new-game start; existing saves can use area selection and reputation progression.
Uses the native mod loader. No required mods. Remove a manually installed copy with the same mod ID so it does not override the Workshop version.
